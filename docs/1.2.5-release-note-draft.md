# CallRise AI 1.2.5 — Privacy fixes

This release fixes two issues in how CallRise AI handles the other party's
words during a call when they haven't consented to being recorded, or
consent was turned off partway through.

**1. "Ask the coach" could still include the other party's words after
consent was turned off.**
Every other place in the app that sends the other party's words to your AI
provider double-checks, right before doing so, that consent is still active.
"Ask the coach" didn't have that check. If you turned off buyer-side
recording mid-call and then used "Ask the coach" afterward, it could still
send words captured earlier in the call to your configured AI provider.
Fixed — it now checks consent the same way every other coaching feature
already does.

**2. The other party's words stayed on your computer indefinitely, even
without consent.**
CallRise AI keeps a behind-the-scenes copy of each call as it happens,
purely so nothing is lost if the app crashes mid-call. This file never left
your computer — it isn't backed up, exported, or sent anywhere — but it
wasn't being cleaned up the way the actual saved call already was: even when
the other party hadn't consented, or you turned recording off, their words
stayed in this backup file in plain text, indefinitely. Fixed — the backup
file is now cleaned up to match what the saved call already shows, both for
calls going forward and for any of these files already on your computer.

**What this means for you:** if you've ever used buyer-capture with consent
declined or turned off mid-call, update to 1.2.5. Nothing to do manually —
both fixes apply automatically the next time you open the app.
