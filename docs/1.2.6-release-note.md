# CallRise AI 1.2.6

**A privacy fix. Please update.**

## What was wrong

When you allow CallRise to record the other person on a call, that permission was staying active after the call ended.

It should end with the call. It didn't — so on your **next** call, the app could start recording the other person again using the permission you gave on the *previous* one. You would not have been asked, and nothing on screen would have looked different.

This affects 1.2.5 and every version before it.

## To be clear about what this was and wasn't

- **Nothing was ever sent anywhere.** This is about audio being captured on your own machine, not about anything leaving it.
- **It needed two calls in the same session.** Closing and reopening the app already cleared the permission — that check was working.
- **Nobody reported this.** It was found during a review of the consent code, and reproduced deliberately before anything was changed.

We're shipping it on its own, ahead of the larger release that was in progress, because a permission that outlives the thing it was granted for is not something to leave sitting in the field while other work finishes.

## What changed

- **The permission now ends when the call ends** — whether you saved that call, abandoned it, or the app closed it for you.
- **Recording can no longer start if saving the permission failed.** Three places could previously begin recording without checking that step succeeded; one of them — the "try again" button after a recording error — wasn't asking for permission at all.

## What has not changed

Everything about how you *give* permission is the same. Consent granted during a call still works exactly as before, including when the app switches audio modes mid-call — that path was specifically tested to make sure this fix didn't break it.

## One thing deliberately left for the next release

The recording check still doesn't verify *which* call a permission belongs to — it only checks that a current one exists. With the permission now ending at the call, there's nothing left over for a later call to pick up, which is what closed this.

Making that check name the specific call is a real improvement, and it lands in the next release. Doing it *now* would have broken recording whenever the app switches audio modes mid-call — trading a silent problem for one that stops a working call in front of your customer. That trade wasn't worth it.

---

*Found and fixed during a systematic review of consent handling. Verified by reproducing the problem through the app's own recording path, fixing it, and confirming the problem no longer reproduces while normal recording — including mid-call mode switches — still works.*
